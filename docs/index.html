How it works
For people who want AI
Open the local chat at localhost:8080. Your prompts never leave your machine — the driver node on your computer handles all readable text. Middle nodes in the chain process only opaque numbers, never text. That is a property of how the work is split, not a promise anyone has to keep.
For people who contribute compute
Your machine holds one slice of a large model. While idle it serves inference requests from the network. It earns compute credits for every request served — a record of work contributed, tracked in an open ledger. Your node pauses the moment you touch the keyboard, and on battery.
What are compute credits? A unit of AI inference delivered by the network on hardware that already existed — like kilowatt-hours on an electricity grid. They have no cash value, there is no exchange and no sale. The full accounting model is open source and auditable by anyone.
